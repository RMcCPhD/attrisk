Overview
Shrinkage via beta ~ normal(0, tau)	Helps prevent overfitting with 18+ covariates
All covariates pre-processed thoughtfully (binary or scaled)	Avoids multicollinearity and parameter explosion
Safe transformation of a_raw → a	Allows for decreasing hazards (as Gompertz requires) without zero/NaN errors
Generated quantities (r_new, p_new, rate)	Enables posterior predictive checks and simulation
Categorical collapsing where needed	Improves identifiability and estimation in sparse covariate strata
Use of expm1(a * dt) / a	Numerically stable and efficient way to compute the Gompertz cumulative hazard
